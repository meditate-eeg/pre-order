How it works

1

Constant Anxiety

It's hard to "turn off" your mind after sirens, shelling, or bad news.

Lack of Focus

When there is finally power and time to work, you need to get things done quickly — but your focus doesn't return.

Fatigue

Even after a short rest, you're left with a sense of emptiness, exhaustion, and mental overload.

Running Out of Resources

In the morning, there's no reserve left to start the day. You keep going — but it's increasingly hard to understand how.

2

Precision brain-activity
technology

The headset reads your brain signals via sensors on your head — as passively as a smartwatch tracks your pulse. No impact on the body, no pain — the device only listens and provides feedback.

Audio Signal

When your attention drifts, the app plays a soft sound. Not to distract, but to remind you: return to the state where you work best.

Recovery Mode

Only have 10 minutes between tasks? The headset helps you reach a state of deep calm faster — instead of just "lying there and thinking."

Offline Operation

The app works without internet. Use it at home, in a shelter, or during a blackout.

Training Over Time

The brain learns through repetition, like any other skill. The more you practice, the faster it returns to calm after stressful moments.

3

What changes
after the first sessions

After a 10-minute session, the headset shows if your brain has actually reached a state of calm and helps you get there via audio feedback. With practice, the brain learns to do this on its own, without cues.

FAQ

Is this a medical device? +

No, it's a tool for attention training and regulation; it does not diagnose or treat. The system helps you learn to manage your brain state but does not replace therapy or medication.

When does the pilot start? +

Tentatively in the next quarter. We will announce the exact date to everyone who has pre-ordered.

What if the pre-order doesn't start? +

You don't pay anything now. We are simply looking for the first interested people. If the project doesn't start, you lose nothing.

Is it safe for children? +

Yes, the device is non-invasive and reads signals from the skin surface. It does not transmit electric current to the brain. Recommended age — from 7 years.

Are there scientific studies? +

Yes. The method of feedback via brain signals has been studied for over 50 years and is used in sports psychology, rehabilitation, and education. We focused on open research from PubMed databases and are ready to share links upon request.

What else can the headset be used for? +

Understanding your internal state, training calm and concentration, for children — focus development, post-stress rehabilitation, meditation, and mindfulness practices.
